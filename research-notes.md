# Reference and Ontario research notes

Reference product: [Willful](https://www.willful.co/). Its public homepage positions online wills as fast, smart, affordable, and Canada-focused. The visible journey emphasizes answering questions, reviewing before payment, printing and signing with instructions, and making updates as life changes. It presents Personal and Professionals pathways, provincial navigation including Ontario, educational resources, pricing, sample documents, reviews, security/privacy, and a sign-up CTA.

Authoritative Ontario source reviewed: [Succession Law Reform Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. S.26](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90s26/v9), which is the statutory source for Ontario succession and will-execution rules. Product signing instructions must be reviewed against the current statute and qualified Ontario counsel before production use.

The product response is deliberately differentiated around nonprofit access, pay-what-you-can funding, explicit suitability screening, privacy-by-default reminders, professional-review escalation, and a more visible software-not-legal-advice boundary. No customer reviews, ratings, or testimonials are fabricated in the product.
